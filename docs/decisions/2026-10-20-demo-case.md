# Demo case: Ollie's, Dublin 2

**Date:** 20 Oct 2026

- **The café:** Ollie's, an invented café off Grafton Street in Dublin 2. Seven staff, open 7am to 8pm, seven days. Every name and number is invented.
- **The owner is role-play.** Nobody is interviewed for real. A second Claude plays Gráinne, the owner, and answers only from her notes (`data/ollies/owner-notes.md`). Everything downstream of the interview is the real product working: the rules really are turned into checks by Claude, and the gate really runs them.
- **Her rules, in her words:** max 40 hours a week; 11 hours off between shifts (Irish working-time law); always two on, three on Saturday morning; a trained barista on every shift; respect when people can't work (Aoife has college on Tuesdays); weekend shifts shared fairly (judged, and labelled as judged).
- **The story:** "Sarah's off sick next week, redo the rota." The assistant drafts, the gate catches what broke in plain words, the assistant fixes it, everything passes, the owner approves, and the run is saved with its evidence page.
